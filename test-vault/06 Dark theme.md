# Dark theme

The plugin follows Obsidian's theme. To switch: **Settings → Appearance → Base color scheme**
(Light / Dark). Boards on the page should change **straight away**, without reloading.
Boards further down that were out of view should have changed too when you scroll to them.

Check the entries below in **both** themes. In dark, also skim [[01 Board positions]],
[[02 Cube and match]], [[05 Invalid input]] (error box) and the "Invalid turn field" entry in
[[10 Regressions]] (yellow warning bar).

In the light theme everything should look exactly as it did before dark theme was added.
